1. What Strider does

Your videos and captions remain entirely yours. We claim no ownership and no licence beyond what is needed to publish them where you asked.

5. Checking your posts went out

Strider checks the destination account before every post and refuses to publish if it does not match the account you set up. Even so, automated publishing can fail — platforms change, connections expire, and services have outages. Please check that important posts published as you intended. Strider records every success and failure so you can see what happened.

10. Plans, paying and cancelling

What Strider costs, and what each plan includes, is set out on the pricing page. Subscriptions are monthly. There is no annual plan.

Basic is free and does not end. It needs no card and no subscription. Paid plans are charged monthly in advance until you cancel.

What a post means. Every plan includes a number of posts a month. One post is one video published to one platform, so a single video sent to four platforms uses four of your posts. A post that fails at the platform does not count against your allowance, and cancelling something you have scheduled returns the post to it.

Introductory prices. Where the pricing page offers a reduced price for a new member’s first months, it applies once per account. When that period ends, your plan continues at the full price shown on the pricing page. We tell you both prices before you subscribe.

Bonus posts are not part of your plan. We may add posts on top of your plan's allowance — for example in your first month. These are a gesture rather than an entitlement, they are shown separately from your plan allowance, and they may change or stop at any time. Your plan's own allowance will not be reduced without notice under section 11.

Scheduled posts need an active plan on the day they publish. Scheduling something further ahead than your plan covers, or beyond the end of a subscription you have cancelled, means it will not go out. Strider tells you this before you cancel.

You can cancel at any time from your account page, with one button. You do not need to contact us, and there is no waiting period and no fee for leaving. Cancelling stops the subscription renewing. You keep your paid plan until the end of the period you have paid for; anything you have scheduled inside that period still publishes. At the end of it, posts scheduled beyond that date are cancelled and your account moves to Basic rather than closing.

When we refund, and what happens to an unused X balance, is set out in full in the refund policy, which forms part of these terms.

X is available on paid plans only, which is why Basic never asks for card details. X charges for every post made to it. Strider passes that charge on with a 15% handling margin added, and X credit is topped up in amounts of £10 or more. No other platform charges per post. The amount is shown before you post and is returned if you cancel a scheduled post. If you move back to Basic, any X credit you hold stays on your account and becomes usable again if you return to a paid plan; if you would rather spend it down, ask us and we will keep X available on your account until the balance reaches zero.
