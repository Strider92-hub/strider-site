Good to know

Facebook — frequently asked questions

Simple answers about using Facebook with Strider.

Can I use Facebook with Strider today?⌄

Facebook is supported by Strider. Public signup is not open yet; contact Strider to ask about early access.

How do I publish a video?⌄

Once your account has access, connect Facebook, upload your finished video, add your caption and settings, then post or choose a publishing time.

Can I schedule videos for later?⌄

Yes. Scheduling is included in the current Facebook integration. Choose a future publishing time from Strider.

Can I post to my personal Facebook profile?⌄

The current Strider integration is for Reels on a Facebook Page you manage, rather than personal profiles.

Do I need to share my Facebook password?⌄

No. Connect through Facebook’s own sign-in. You do not give your social media password to Strider.

Which plan includes Facebook?⌄

Choose at least two supported platforms. The two-platform plan is £10/month with a 28-day free trial; three platforms cost £15/month and all five are £20/month during the launch offer. Other plans charge from the start. Platform availability still applies.
