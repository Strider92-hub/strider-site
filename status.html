Status

Last updated 15 September 2026

Strider is not yet open for public sign-up. All five platforms are now built and publishing. Two of them — YouTube and X — are ready for anyone to connect. TikTok, Facebook and Instagram work, but each platform reviews an app before it will allow accounts other than the developer’s own, and those reviews are still outstanding. This page will report real operational status once there is a live service behind it.

What the two columns mean. “Built and publishing” means Strider has posted and scheduled real video to that platform’s live API from the live server — not in a test, and not as a mock. “Open to your account” is a separate question, and it is not ours to answer: each platform decides when an app may connect accounts belonging to people other than its developer. Where it says not yet, the work is finished and the platform has not finished reviewing.

None of this yet means a live service with paying users on it.

Past incidents

None. There is no live service yet, so there is nothing to report.

Reporting a problem

Strider is not open to the public, so there is no support queue yet. If you are testing Strider and something is not working, email hello@getstrider.co.uk with what you expected to happen and what happened instead.

Why this page looks thin. A status page is only worth anything if it tells the truth. Until there is a hosted service with users on it, the honest thing to report is that there has been nothing to go wrong yet. When platforms open and accounts arrive, outages will appear here.
