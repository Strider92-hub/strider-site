Strider

Social media scheduling for creators.

Strider schedules short-form video. You line up a week of clips once, and Strider publishes them to your own accounts at the times you chose. YouTube is built and working today. TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook and X are being added.

Strider is in development and not yet open to the public. YouTube publishing is built and working; Facebook, Instagram, TikTok and X are not connected yet. There is no sign-up and nothing to pay for. What follows describes what Strider is being built to do — the platform support page separates what exists today from what is planned.

If you are a creator who would like to try it early, email hello@getstrider.co.uk and you will be told when places open.

What it is being built to do

  • Schedule one video to several platforms at once, with the caption each platform needs
  • Plan weeks ahead rather than days
  • Change a scheduled post without deleting and re-uploading it
  • Post the first comment automatically on the platforms that allow it — useful for linking your longer video. YouTube and TikTok do not permit this; the platform support page says which do
  • Load a whole batch of clips in one go

Who it is for

Individual creators. Strider is deliberately not built for agencies, teams or client approval workflows — there are good tools for that already, and they are priced for companies rather than for people.

The pricing intention: one price, however many platforms. Most scheduling tools charge per connected account, so posting to five platforms costs five times as much. Strider will be priced per creator — posting everywhere is the point of it, not an upgrade. Nothing is on sale yet and there is no price list; this is the plan, not an offer.

Your accounts stay yours

Strider publishes to accounts you connect yourself, and only to those accounts. It checks which account it is about to publish to before every single post, and refuses to publish if it does not match the one you set up. Nothing is posted anywhere you did not choose.

We never see your password. Connecting an account uses each platform's own sign-in, and you can disconnect at any time — from inside Strider, or from the platform's own settings.
