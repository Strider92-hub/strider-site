Refund Policy

Strider · last updated 14 September 2026

This policy sits alongside our Terms of Service. Nothing in it removes any right you have under UK consumer law.

Cancelling

You can cancel at any time, from your account page, with one button. You do not need to email us, telephone us, or wait for anything. Cancelling stops the subscription renewing. You keep Strider until the end of the period you have already paid for.

The 28-day free trial

The free trial applies to the two-platform plan only. The three-platform, five-platform and annual plans are charged when you sign up.

Where the trial does apply, nothing is charged during it. We email you before it ends. If you cancel at any point before the trial finishes, you are not charged at all.

Monthly plans

If you cancel a monthly plan, it does not renew and you keep Strider until the end of the month you have paid for. We do not charge a fee for cancelling.

Annual plans

An annual plan is paid up front for twelve months.

  • Within 14 days of first subscribing, you can cancel for a full refund.
  • After that, the plan runs for the twelve months you have paid for and then stops if you have cancelled. We do not refund the remaining months.
  • At each renewal you have a further cooling-off period, and cancelling within it is refunded in proportion to what is left.

We say this plainly because an annual plan is a genuine twelve-month commitment and we would rather you knew that before buying than afterwards.

Your X balance

The X balance is not part of your subscription. It is money you add to cover what X charges Strider for each post you make there, passed on at cost.

  • Credit you have not spent is refundable on request while your account is open — email us.
  • Credit held against a scheduled post is returned automatically if you cancel that post, or if it fails.
  • Credit spent on a post that went out is not refundable, because X has already charged us for it.
  • If you delete your account, any remaining credit goes with it. Your account page shows the amount before you confirm.

If something goes wrong at our end

If Strider fails to publish something because of a fault on our side, tell us and we will put it right — including refunding the period affected where that is the fair answer. We would rather do that than argue about it.

How to ask

Email hello@getstrider.co.uk from the address on your account. We aim to answer within three working days. Refunds go back to the card you paid with, through Paddle, who handle payments for Strider.
