The founding price is 60% off and is limited to the first fifty accounts. When they are gone, they are gone. Availability will be confirmed when signup opens; this preview is not connected to the account count.

The founding price covers the first year only. You pay £96 for your first twelve months. After that the plan renews at the ordinary annual price of £240 a year, which is the same as paying £20 a month for all five platforms. We will email you before the first renewal so the change never arrives as a surprise, and you can cancel at any time before it from your account page.
What "all five" covers. The all-five price covers the five platforms in the current product offering: YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and X. If Strider adds another platform later it may be priced separately. Your existing plan will not change price without us telling you first.

X posting charges include a 15% markup

X is the one platform that charges per post, and it charges Strider directly for using its API. Strider adds 15% to the underlying X posting charge to help cover payment-processing and service costs. The full charge is shown before you post.

The minimum top-up is £10. Top-ups are paid in GBP; X credit is shown in USD. The exchange rate and USD credit must be confirmed at checkout before you pay. Each post draws from your X balance. The cost is shown on the posting screen before you press anything, the amount is held when you schedule, and it is given back if you cancel. If your balance runs out, Strider stops rather than running up a bill.

Questions

Can I change how many platforms I am on? Yes, from your account page. A change takes effect from your next payment.

What happens if I cancel? You keep Strider until the end of the period you have paid for, and it does not renew. See the refund policy.

Is there VAT on top? Payments are handled by Paddle. Any applicable tax is shown at checkout before you confirm.
