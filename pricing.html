Your first month, explained.

Basic gives you 10 posts a month and 500MB of storage, free, for as long as you want it. Your first month carries a bonus on top — 30 posts and 1GB — so you can try Strider properly. The bonus is a one-off welcome, once per account, and we email you before it runs out. Nothing is deleted when it does: you keep every video, you just upload again once you are back under 500MB.

Your launch price stays yours.

Join Pro during the launch offer and keep £15 a month for as long as the subscription continues. When the offer closes, new subscriptions are £30. If you cancel and come back, the price available then applies. Bonus posts we add on top of your plan are a gesture rather than part of it, and can change.

Basic does not end. When the welcome bonus runs out you stay on Basic, free, with 10 posts a month and 500MB of storage. There is no trial to expire and no card to cancel. We email you before the bonus runs out so it never arrives as a surprise.
Nothing you have uploaded is ever deleted to enforce a limit. If your first month leaves you above 500MB, your videos stay exactly where they are and anything already scheduled still posts. You simply cannot upload anything new until you are back under the limit.
What counts as a post. One post is one video published to one platform. Sending a single video to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram and Facebook uses four of your posts. A post that fails at the platform does not count, and cancelling something you have scheduled gives the post back.

X posting charges include a 15% markup

X is available on Pro only, which is why Basic never asks for card details. X is also the one platform that charges per post, and it charges Strider directly for using its API. Strider adds 15% to the underlying X posting charge to help cover payment-processing and service costs. The full charge is shown before you post.

If you move from Pro back to Basic, your X credit is not lost. The balance stays on your account and becomes usable again the moment you return to Pro. The other four platforms do not charge per post. Only X does, and only because X does.

Questions

Can I change plan? Yes, from your account page. Moving up takes effect straight away; moving down takes effect from your next payment.

What happens if I cancel? You keep Pro until the end of the period you have paid for, and it does not renew. Anything you have scheduled inside that period still posts; anything scheduled after it is cancelled, and your account moves to Basic rather than closing. See the refund policy.

Is there VAT on top? Payments are handled by Paddle. Any applicable tax is shown at checkout before you confirm.
