What "all five" covers. The all-five price covers the five platforms Strider supports today: YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, Instagram and X. If Strider adds another platform later it may be priced separately. Your existing plan will not change price without us telling you first.

X charges for every post, and we pass it on at cost

X is the one platform that charges per post, and it charges Strider directly for using its API. That cost is passed on to you exactly as billed, with nothing added.

You add credit to an X balance and each post draws from it. The cost is shown on the posting screen before you press anything, the amount is held when you schedule, and it is given back if you cancel. If your balance runs out, Strider stops rather than running up a bill.

The other four platforms do not charge per post. Only X does, and only because X does.
