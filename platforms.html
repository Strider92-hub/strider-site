Platform support

What is built, what is planned, and what no tool can do. Last updated 1 September 2026.

Strider is in development and not yet open to the public. Only YouTube is built. This page separates what exists today from what is planned, because a support page is worth nothing if it describes things that are not there.

Built today

PlatformVideoSchedulingFirst commentNotes
YouTube Yes Yes No Shorts and standard uploads. You choose public, unlisted or private. Scheduled videos are uploaded straight away as private, and YouTube publishes them itself at your chosen time. First comments are not possible — see below.

Planned, not yet built

These are the platforms Strider is being built towards. None of them works yet. What each row describes is what the platform's own interface allows, and therefore what Strider will be able to do once it is connected.

PlatformVideoSchedulingFirst commentWhat the platform allows
Facebook Planned Planned Planned Reels to a Page you manage. Facebook can hold a post and publish it itself, up to about 75 days ahead.
Instagram Planned Planned Planned Reels, to a business account linked to a Facebook Page. Instagram has no scheduling of its own, so the timing has to come from Strider.
TikTok Planned Planned Not possible TikTok has no scheduling of its own, so the timing has to come from Strider.
X Planned Planned Planned X has no scheduling of its own. It also now charges for API access, so it is the lowest priority of the four.
Why "the timing has to come from Strider" matters. YouTube and Facebook accept a future time and publish it themselves, so a scheduled post goes out whether or not anything else is running. Instagram, TikTok and X do not offer that at all — something has to be awake and send the post at the moment it is due. That difference is the reason Strider is being built as a hosted service rather than something that runs on your own computer.

Things no tool can do

These are platform limits, not Strider's. Any tool claiming otherwise is worth a second look.
